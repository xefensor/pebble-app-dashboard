Your current activity, notifications and chosen pins in one Pebble overview.

Open Dashboard when you want a quick look at what is happening. Its overview brings together one activity, a notification panel and two pinned items. Choose useful readings and shortcuts, then open the full collections when you need more.

- Hold Up to browse the available Activities.
- Open Pinned to see your full collection of pins.
- Choose what a pin displays, what Select does and which app Hold Select opens, where supported by the source.
- Keep complete values and units together.
- Choose Small, Medium, Large or Follow Pebble text, and a light, dark or following appearance. Available in 17 languages.

Activities are ongoing updates from compatible sources. For example, start a countdown in Timer and open Dashboard to see its remaining time. Dashboard has its own activity order. Open Timer to pause or resume the countdown; Dashboard does not provide those controls.

Shared activities and readings need the official Pebble phone app's plugin support, source permissions and a connected phone. Music and notification integration requires Android. Available actions depend on the source; missing or expired information is shown as unavailable.

Free. For Pebble Time 2 and Round 2.
