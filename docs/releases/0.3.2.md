# Dashboard 0.3.2 local candidate

Reconnect recovers source subscriptions once and gives their bindings new
versions. Old confirmations cannot execute against a reconnected binding.
Late refresh reads cannot replace newer subscription data, expiry or permission
withdrawal, and overlapping refresh requests share one read plus one follow-up.
Stopped/disconnected sources no longer restart from duplicate connection events
or late discovery callbacks. Hiding Music avoids its subscription unless an
explicit pin still reads that source.
Hidden activity provider UUIDs also stop their shared-host subscriptions while
ordinary pinned readings from those providers remain independent.

Static local/action views skip unchanged heartbeat frames. Phone and watch
expiry timers keep unchanged deadlines instead of repeatedly cancelling and
allocating timers. Live leases, source expiry and command confirmation remain
enforced. An unavailable reading's independent capabilities also expire at the
exact native deadline. A countdown paints 00:00 once, then waits for owner state
without per-second redraws. A requested item omitted during LOADING stays inert
until its exact identity returns.

The existing Snapshot/settings design, UUID and DSH1 protocol remain unchanged.
Activities 0.4.3 is pinned with reviewed source/hash provenance. This candidate
has not been published. Validation and measured work counts are in
[the QA record](../qa/0.3.2/README.md); these are not physical-watch battery tests.
