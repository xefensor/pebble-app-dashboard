# Dashboard support

Report problems at [pebble-app-dashboard issues](https://github.com/xefensor/pebble-app-dashboard/issues). Include the app version, watch model and firmware, phone model and Pebble app version, the steps to reproduce, and what you expected. Remove private source text and identifiers from screenshots.

The release being prepared targets Time 2 and Round 2 with SDK 4.44. Duo is deferred. Round 2 checks use the emulator; physical Time 2 and Android checks remain a publication gate. Source tests, native emulator runs, simulated phone bridges and real-device checks are reported separately.

Download published PBWs and checksums from [GitHub releases](https://github.com/xefensor/pebble-app-dashboard/releases). Local candidates are not published releases. This project does not require a separate companion app or developer account.
