Tap Music for playback controls or Kitchen light to toggle it. Hold Select on Timer to open Timer. In the full lists, hold Up / Down to scroll. Holds take 0.6 seconds.

Try it: on Snapshot, hold Down to open Pinned. Release, then hold Down again to scroll through the list. Back restores your original selection. In Music, Up / Down stays with its controls. Tap/hold ↑ ↓ and Enter / Space also work; Esc returns. W / S / H simulate holding Up / Down / Select.

Useful actions on tap

Music opens playback controls. Kitchen light toggles; Find phone rings. Weather and the current Timer summary have no tap action. Nothing opens just to enlarge the same content. Timer Pause/Resume is deferred.

Associated app on hold

The footer names the available destination, such as Timer, Music or Weather. Items without a destination do nothing on hold. Notifications open directly on tap. Swapping activity order preserves your selection and Music control focus.
